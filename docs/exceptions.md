# Exceptions

Rules from the [engineering standards](https://github.com/get-latest/company/blob/main/engineering/standards/engineering-standards.md#exceptions)
that this repo does not meet yet, or does not meet on purpose. Each has a reason, a scope and an
expiry. Reaching an expiry means fixing it or re-recording it here with a new date and a stated
reason. A one-off exception for a single commit goes in that commit as an
`Exception: <rule> - <why> - expires <YYYY-MM-DD>` trailer instead.

The approver, for now, is Matt. Measured counts are from the audit of 2026-09-28 and are
re-measured in [enforcement-matrix.md](enforcement-matrix.md).
